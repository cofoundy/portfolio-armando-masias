About Me

I'm a deckhand and tender driver who has crewed eight vessels — from private superyachts to commercial ships — and I know what a captain needs before being asked. My specialty is tender operations and guest transfers: moving owners and guests safely while keeping the exterior in show-ready condition. I pair a high-end hospitality background with hands-on seamanship — docking, line handling, teak and stainless restoration, and full deck upkeep to superyacht standards. I'm multilingual (English, French, Spanish, Portuguese), an asset in the US and Mediterranean fleets, and I bring the reliability and discipline captains have put in writing. Based in Fort Lauderdale and US work-ready with a B1/B2 visa valid to 2034 and a current ENG1 medical, I can step aboard and contribute from day one.
